# Diablo III PC — R-01, first evidence pass

Owner scope: first-time Campaign **and** Adventure Mode. Read 2026-10-09. Versions: undated Blizzard class guides and historical 2.7.4 announcement. Early unlock detail reaches L2; timed onboarding, loot rates and visual flows remain Q. Sources and precise read ranges are in [SOURCES.csv](SOURCES.csv).

## Findings

Blizzard's [Wizard progression](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/wizard/progression) and [Barbarian progression](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/barbarian/progression) both interleave new abilities with modifiers. Each class's first five active abilities arrive at levels 1–5; the first rune options at 6–7 and first passive choices at 10. Wizard later gets Meteor at 25 and Black Hole at 61; Barbarian gets Whirlwind at 20 and Avalanche at 61. These are **availability levels**, not elapsed minutes or proof of equipped-slot counts. [S; D3-01–04; D3-ACT-W/B, D3-PROG-W/B]

The [2.7.4 announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23816414/season-27-lights-calling-preview) removes Campaign completion as an Adventure access requirement. Its Season Journey rewards chapters with pieces of a class set. Historical progression paths must therefore be separated from a player's chosen first experience. [S; D3-05; D3-274]

## Application to Hearthfall [P]

The useful comparison is a cadence of new verbs, modifiers and longer goals. Our audit shows all actives available by 12 and all runes by 21 [M; HF-01]. That establishes an availability contrast, not evidence that players are bored or that unlocks should simply be delayed. Preserve current skills while measuring actual early-session choices. Do not copy D3 levels, lore, art or set multipliers.
