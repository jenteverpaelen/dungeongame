# R-10 — ARPG/MMO case studies

Read 2026-10-09. Partial L1/L2. Separate documented features, developer explanations, player criticism and measured outcomes.

- [HS-STORE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/269210/Hero_Siege/) advertises skill/sub-skill trees, a market and an in-game mechanics journal. The same page lists paid classes/storage while asserting no sold advantages. Record the tension; do not turn the publisher's fairness claim into a verified conclusion.
- [ROTMG-STORE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/200210/Realm_of_the_Mad_God_Exalt/) documents co-op, guilds, permadeath and persistent pet development; it discloses chance-based purchases. It does not establish server capacity, tick rate or retention.
- [WOL-END](https://wolcengame.com/the-future-of-the-game/) announces an end to development/support and planned multiplayer closure beginning September 3, 2024, with single-player remaining. An announcement does not prove the actual shutdown date or explain failure causally.

**Inference:** Hearthfall needs a save/export and operating-cost plan before depending on permanent online availability. A mechanics journal could reduce external-guide dependence. Permadeath, paid storage or an auction house are not automatically suitable.
