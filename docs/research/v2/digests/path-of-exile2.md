# Path of Exile 2 — decision digest

2026-10-09, C063. **PoE2 only. December2024 Mercenary footage is historical; later patch rules stay separate.** [Dossier](../R04-path-of-exile.md), [versions](../VERSIONS.md), [claims](../CLAIMS.csv).

**What the player decides.** Gemcutting presents skill choices; the Skills pane shows assigned abilities and support spaces; the support picker changes suggestions with the selected skill. Tooltips expose applicability, attribute needs, benefits and penalties. A later Skills row identifies an attached support and changed capacity usage (POE2-10, POE2-11, POE2-12). That sequence demonstrates visible contexts, not the unseen input or computed outcome.

**Useful pattern for Hearthfall [inference].** Show why a modifier fits, what it changes and any trade-off beside the current ability. A disabled modifier should name its unmet requirement and leave other valid choices understandable. Later notes describe support compatibility at selection, before/after gem values and recovery scoped to an invalid support rather than the whole skill (POE2-01, POE2-02, POE2-05). These are contracts to investigate in existing rune/tier UI, not a new gem architecture.

**Avoid transferring.** The video's one-copy support restriction was removed in0.3. Do not combine it with later tiers or PoE1 sockets. Price colour and a low displayed balance do not prove actual rejection. The Mercenary's controls, manual combat and gem costs do not establish a fit for Hearthfall's automatic combat (POE2-01, POE2-14).

**Still unknown.** Current invalid-support recovery and complete unlock/cost order; actual assignment inputs; computed modifiers; durable results; player comprehension and comparable pace. Presenter obstruction and changing source resolution limit geometric conclusions.

**Next action.** For future objectives, require the authoritative action itself: patch reports describe completion without the intended interaction or boss kill (POE2-04). C040/C041 already distinguish Hearthfall's event, proximity and ownership boundaries; durable journal/reward integration remains to be built and tested. No new gem tree, manual combat, penalty or balance target is selected.
