# Legends of Idleon — decision digest

2026-10-09, C063. **Official loop descriptions, secondary guides and historical recorded pixels; current rules remain partial.** [Dossier](../R02-idleon.md), [claims](../CLAIMS.csv), [sampled frames](../TIMELINE-FRAMES.json).

**What the player decides.** Official descriptions connect complementary characters with combat, gathering, crafting and away progress. Recorded talent panes expose current/next effects and a point budget; production and equipment crafting have different contexts. Cards distinguish collection from equipped contribution. Away Info forecasts differ from AFK Gains elapsed-away totals and the later acquisition display (IDLE-01, IDLE-09, IDLE-10, IDLE-11, IDLE-12).

**Useful pattern for Hearthfall [inference].** Explain unattended rewards using their actual basis and result. An estimate, a return summary, possession and an equipped benefit should not share an ambiguous success message. Small objectives can introduce existing combat, equipment and talent actions; the secondary quest sequence supports investigating that approach, not adopting its exact rewards (IDLE-03). Hearthfall's measured offline rules and finite inventory must determine its implementation.

**Avoid transferring.** Multi-character production does not prove that adding alts improves retention. An open-client event is not the general offline system (IDLE-04). The developer described removing an incentive to stockpile; copying the reward alone could recreate the behavior they were correcting (IDLE-05). Two guides report class choice at10, while footage shows a Warrior at8: neither establishes a current minimum (IDLE-02, IDLE-13).

**Still unknown.** Current AFK formula/cap and ownership boundaries; exact promotion rule; ordinary first-account times; current empty/full inventory and reward-retry behavior. A matching acquisition notice does not prove a durable, exactly-once grant.

**Next action.** Carry estimate/result/ownership distinctions into the future offline-reward and objective contracts. Verify retries, overflow and save/restore locally before expanding away rewards. No account-wide alt economy, card system, production board, paid slot or new offline ratio is selected.
