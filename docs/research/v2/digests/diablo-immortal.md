# Diablo Immortal — decision digest

2026-10-09, C066. **Historical publisher statements/changes plus attributed consumer criticism; no current-client or legal determination.** [Dossier](../R10-case-studies.md), [claims](../CLAIMS.csv).

**What the player decides.** The alpha model separates gear from paid Crests/reforging; later notes revise bundle sizes and reduce Warband restrictions in response to feedback (IMM-01,IMM-02,IMM-04).

**Useful pattern for Hearthfall [inference].** Make real eligibility,ownership and costs understandable. Test grouping requirements against players who cannot assemble a fixed team.

**Avoid transferring.** The Consumer Council criticises layered premium conversion for obscuring value (IMM-03). This is an attributed analysis,not a court ruling. No payment system is proposed.

**Still unknown.** Current purchase rules,actual spending outcomes,causal retention and whether each reported friction was resolved remain unknown.

**Next action.** Keep Hearthfall’s no-payment scope. For later social systems,distinguish membership from momentary participation and explain why entry fails; no copied currency,gate or reward quantity.

Sources: [Publisher model](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23580185/our-free-to-play-approach-and-values); [consumer report](https://storage02.forbrukerradet.no/media/2024/09/getting-played-2024-compressed-komprimert-sept24-1.pdf).
