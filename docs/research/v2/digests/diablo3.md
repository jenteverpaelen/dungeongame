# Diablo III PC — decision digest

2026-10-09, C063. Both Campaign and Adventure Mode. **Historical/source evidence; current client and ordinary first-account pace remain unverified.** [Dossier](../R01-diablo3.md), [claims](../CLAIMS.csv), [sampled frames](../TIMELINE-FRAMES.json).

**What the player decides.** Published class progression interleaves new abilities, runes and passives. These are different choices from assigning the available action slots. Campaign footage shows directed objectives and return-for-reward prompts; Adventure footage shows activity selection on the waypoint map, target/count objectives and an artisan loop. Artisan rank, Cube collection and character level are separate progress tracks. Evidence: D3-07, D3-11, D3-29, D3-32, D3-33, D3-34.

**Useful pattern for Hearthfall [inference].** Make the next actionable choice understandable in its existing panel: what becomes available, what can be equipped, and what the choice changes. A future objective should point to a working action and retain its identity through the world, tracker and reward conversation. A future loadout must state its component scope: the historical Armory included gear, gems, skills/runes and Cube choices but excluded Paragon (D3-09). These are information contracts, not a proposal to copy layouts or unlock levels.

**Avoid transferring.** The Campaign character already has Paragon100; Adventure starts with established-account footage, then shows a level1 resource jump and changing difficulty. Neither is a fresh-account timing target (D3-28, D3-31). Later ability availability does not justify removing skills from existing Hearthfall characters. A developer's projectile-performance explanation is a useful profiling question, not proof of a frame-rate improvement here (D3-08).

**Still unknown.** Comparable progression times; first useful-item distributions; current input/error/transaction behavior; first-time player understanding. Displayed historical slot locks are observed, but their complete current enforcement is not.

**Next action.** Keep current availability. Use the existing skill/tier audit and first-decision trace to design a bounded explanation or objective, then test its actual result and recovery. No seasonal acceleration, new action-slot gate, loadout system or reward schedule is selected by this digest.
