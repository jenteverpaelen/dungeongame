# Path of Exile 1 — decision digest

2026-10-09, C063. **PoE1 only. Historical March2026 footage and separately versioned official rules.** [Dossier](../R04-path-of-exile.md), [versions](../VERSIONS.md), [claims](../CLAIMS.csv).

**What the player decides.** Skill gems as items, their equipment requirements and their action-bar summaries are separate contexts. The observed passive tree explicitly labels an allocation as unconfirmed and offers apply/cancel. Quest/map prompts lead toward a reward choice beside inventory; vendor sale separates the offered items from expected payment and acceptance (POE-10, POE-11, POE-12, POE-14).

**Useful pattern for Hearthfall [inference].** Label previewed changes before commitment and expose the relevant requirement where the decision happens. A future multi-step reward or crafting choice must define when the server commits it and how the player recovers after interruption. Progressively available help is a documented pattern, but its effectiveness must be tested with Hearthfall players (POE-03). Existing rune/tier panels provide a smaller surface for these ideas.

**Avoid transferring.** Official overview text can be stale: the no-gold description conflicts with later patch notes. March socket-colour instructions predate3.29's changed rule (POE-02, POE-05). Genesis refunds and Bloodline replacement conditions do not settle every passive refund rule (POE-04). A large tree, currencies or socket restrictions are not a requirement for build depth here.

**Still unknown.** Current ordinary gem acquisition and invalid-support recovery; actual point/reward commits; ownership after restart; complete refund economy; useful-loot and progression distributions. The observed stash's tab count does not establish free entitlements (POE-13).

**Next action.** Use explicit pending/committed/cancelled states in the objective/reward design, backed by existing local crash/retry research. Preserve Hearthfall's current free tier refund until a separately supported decision changes it. No imported tree, gem system, fee, trading model or numerical cadence is selected.
