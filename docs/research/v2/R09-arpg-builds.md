# R-09 — Last Epoch, Grim Dawn, Torchlight Infinite

Read 2026-10-09. Partial L1/L2. Torchlight II has its own R-21 dossier.

## Read evidence

[LE-SKILL](https://support.lastepoch.com/hc/en-us/articles/46363203944859-Skill-Specialization), updated 2026-02-12, documents up to five specialized skills, individual skill trees, and respecialization that uses a minimum skill level and possible catch-up experience. Distinguish character progression, skill progression and respec recovery.

[GD-FEAT](https://www.grimdawn.com/guide/about/major-features/) describes dual masteries and shrine-earned Devotion, plus faction-linked rewards. Its six-class wording is legacy base-game evidence, not a current expansion count.

[TI-VORAX](https://torchlight.xd.com/us/ep13) advertises an auto-casting activation medium after story completion and once-per-account selected rewards. It also promotes random draws. The year and current season cannot be established from that page alone; a promotional grant is not the normal reward economy.

## Hearthfall inference

Build identity can come from a few meaningful choices at different layers; more trees are not automatically more depth. Any respec model should state what is retained, what must be re-earned and how an ineffective build recovers. Account grants need a reliable account identity first. No gacha or paid progression is proposed.

## Charter gaps

Mastery/passive details, crafting risk, loot filters, first-session UI, endgame loops and Infinite's full monetization remain unfinished. No numerical target or new talent tree is selected from this pass.
