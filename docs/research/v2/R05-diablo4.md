# R-05 — Diablo IV

Read 2026-10-09. Partial L2. Base game, expansions and seasons must be separated; this dossier does not establish the latest installed October build.

## What the evidence supports

[D4-304](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24271857/diablo-iv-patch-notes-3-0) reports 2026 fixes for interrupted reward claims, objectives completed before their expected state, repeated portals and lost socketed items. These are useful concrete regression scenarios. They do not prove any particular retention outcome.

[D4-313](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24287406/diablo-iv-patch-notes-3-1) changes item modification rules in 3.1.0 and later clarifies a Cube recipe's name to match its operation. Treat economy and tooltip evidence as versioned together. Its later notes address spell effects obscuring an encounter. Visual spectacle and target readability can conflict.

## Hearthfall application — inference, not copied design

Before adding quest rewards or crafting, test disconnect/retry, two players at different objective states, early kills, inventory-full claims and socket preservation. A displayed cost or eligibility rule should come from the same shared data used by the server. Preserve the current visual style; improve legibility through settings and clear state feedback where measured.

## Additional information channels — launch-era evidence

Blizzard's [accessibility introduction](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23954932/combatting-demons-with-accessibility-in-diablo-iv), written before the June6 launch, describes configurable gear-drop/ground audio by rarity, item-hover cues, font sizes and screen narration in its Vision Assistance section. This is a published feature description, not our observed playthrough or a current-patch verification; embedded videos were not inspected. C049's written quality labels are a separate Hearthfall inference from GAG/XAG guidance, not a claim that D4 uses that exact presentation.
