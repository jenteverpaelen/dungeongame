## Guidance applied [S] and decisions still needed [Q]

[EDPB basics](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-protection-basics_en) supports purpose-specific collection, security and retention limits. This table records existing behavior; it does not assign a legal basis or make an indefinite-retention policy acceptable.

[EDPB rights](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en) distinguishes access, erasure and conditional portability, identity checks where doubt exists, and protection of other people's information. A raw save download is not the complete rights process. Name-only login cannot justify a public export/delete endpoint. Define identity verification, request handling, exceptions and backup/restore effects before enabling it.

[OWASP logging](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html) calls for excluding secrets, safely encoding bounded diagnostic context, protecting access and managing copies/retention. A character ID may still identify a player; hashing it is not automatically anonymization. Do not collect extra profile/contact fields solely because a dependency supports them.

Still needed: operator/controller and hosting recipients; existing-player ownership; age/audience and support model; purpose/legal-basis decisions; access controls; specific retention/recovery requirements; full dependency/network and log audit; rights workflow including active sessions, failed-write memory, quarantine, backups and restored old state. **No retention number, legal age, export/deletion endpoint or new data collection is selected here.** Account/library prototypes and other roadmap work can continue independently.
