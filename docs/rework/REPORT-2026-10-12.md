## What you can try (10 minutes)

1. **Your hero:** the HUD orbs, XP bar and prompts look the same but no longer repaint the screen every frame.
2. **Skills (K):** 12 skills per class, 5 runes each, a skill tree per skill (Specialization tab; 50 points at level 70,
   max 10 per tree), 14 passives. New skills unlock at 16, 20, 26, 32, 40 and 50 (F2 → Level +10 to see them).
3. **Build tools:**
   * hold **Alt** while a tooltip is open (or Settings → Display → *Advanced tooltips*) for exact numbers;
   * **Character → Damage** for the per-skill damage breakdown (pin one setup to compare);
   * the **training dummy** in town for a 30/60/120 s DPS test;
   * **5 skill loadouts** (free respec, switch in town);
   * **build codes**: Skills window → Copy, paste in chat, others click *Apply*.
4. **Weapons:** every weapon type now has 7 maker series (Millwright → Kilnfolk → Brinekeeper → Warden → Array →
   Faultstone → Reliquary) with their own effects. Old art: Settings → Display → *Weapon art: Previous*.
5. **Items:** Kindled (extra ember affix) and Fabled (max rolls + one Inscription) replace Ancient/Primal; 80 item types
   (each with its own drawing), 55 affixes, 122 legendaries, 15 sets, a ring that lets a set need one piece less.

## Honest gaps

* **Endgame power dropped a lot** (full sets ×13–29 instead of up to ×387) so legendary builds can compete. The
  difficulty ladder (Ordeal) has **not** been re-fitted yet; Ordeal II rifts no longer finish in 12 minutes.
* **Set vs legendary balance is not right yet:** the shared legendaries are a bit too strong (legendary-only kits often
  beat sets), a few named weapons dominate their skill, the mage frost set wins too often. Measured; next is a balance pass.
* **Levelling pace** passes but sits right at the target from level 25 (6.5 s per champion vs 6.4 s); the Warrior is the
  slowest class there (9 s). The Ranger has a few maps where it dies repeatedly in endgame rifts.
* Nobody has played the new classes by hand yet; all numbers come from bots and simulations.
