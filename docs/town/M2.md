# M2 — Gate 2: playable services blockout

Gate 1 approved; M1 tagged town-m1 (127b5d5). Gate 2 was subsequently approved by “start the look slice!!”; see M3-SLICE.md for the next review. Results below remain the M2 checkpoint record.

## What works

The authored town includes nine solid building masses, a walkable inn doorway recess, 19 circle-clear routes, a polygon minimap and F3 collision/baseline/interaction diagnostics. Blacksmith, Jeweler, Mystic, Cube, Stash, Waypoint, Rift Obelisk and Paragon shrine are physical services; the upper court has three damageable training dummies, including one elite. Every service mutation checks current server position, correct role, live state and static-geometry line of sight. Waypoint travel also accepts the matching authored exit; rift entry also accepts the actual open rift portal. Field/rift return-home behavior is retained.

The 60-slot stash belongs to each character. Transfers use item IDs, preserve all item properties, reject retries/duplicates/full destinations, and survive logout/relogin. Migration adds the array to old saves without dropping existing items. Existing Cube levels, costs, XP and progression stay in place; the same panel is filtered by artisan. U works only beside Cube. P can still inspect Paragon anywhere; mutations require the shrine. Open service panels may remain visible when walking away, but server operations are refused.

## Route measurements

Nominal = authored polyline length / 250 u/s. Observed = performance.now() around the browser's normal input/prediction loop on one local run, including arrival steering. Zero equipment/Paragon movement bonus, no dash. These are not D3 timings or a promise of exact wall-clock speed. Many observed times are shorter than nominal. **Inference:** the unchanged server input scheduler can replay the previous input when a queue is empty and consume two queued inputs on a later tick, which can affect real-time traversal. This has not been isolated with a dedicated timing experiment; do not change netcode under the town scope or label these as 250 u/s stopwatch proof.

No 100-player render/performance claim is made for this milestone.

## Walkthrough

Move with WASD/arrows; E at each service; U beside Cube; I for bag; F3 for geometry; Esc closes panels. From the plaza follow the lane west around the inn to the shrine and upper training court; the pier forks left. The southern approach is reached through the eastern gate, not straight south from the plaza. Inn/Forge full interiors remain optional M5.

The local review server uses port 2577 and a separate temporary DATA_DIR. It is a test character, not an owner's save. Review the layout and physical service flow at Gate 2; the later look gate remains required.
